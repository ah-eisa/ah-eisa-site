August 2025 · ETF Markets
Morningstar ETF Flows Report

ETF Flows 2025 — Where Smart Money Moved

2025 saw record ETF inflows driven by gold, value, and short-term bond funds. iShares and Dimensional led market-share gains as investors rotated from high-growth tech to income-oriented strategies in anticipation of rate cuts and dollar weakness. Active ETFs expanded rapidly, capturing assets from mutual funds in both U.S. and international categories — a structural shift worth watching for UAE-based portfolio allocators.
