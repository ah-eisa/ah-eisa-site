Morningstar Summary

Q4 2025 Stock Market Outlook — No Margin for Error

The U.S. market traded at a 3% premium to fair value — a rare event seen only 15% of the time since 2010. Roughly 40% of market cap is concentrated in 10 AI mega-caps, leaving little room for error if AI growth fades. Small-caps remained 16% undervalued and offered the most attractive entry point, while value stocks traded 3% below fair value. Sectors including real estate, energy, and healthcare remained undervalued — the right positioning is to focus there and avoid over-exposure to AI growth equities.

5 Common Portfolio Mistakes — And How to Fix Them

Common structural pitfalls include: portfolio sprawl across too many accounts; redundant holdings in overlapping ETFs and stocks; holding under-performing funds too long; misaligned asset allocation near retirement; and sub-optimal tax location of assets. Morningstar ETF Flows Report

ETF Flows 2025 — Where Smart Money Moved

2025 saw record ETF inflows driven by gold, value, and short-term bond funds. iShares and Dimensional led market-share gains as investors rotated from high-growth tech to income-oriented strategies in anticipation of rate cuts and dollar weakness. Active ETFs expanded rapidly, capturing assets from mutual funds in both U.S. and international categories — a structural shift worth watching for UAE-based portfolio allocators.
