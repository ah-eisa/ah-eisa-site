Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026  ·  Last updated: 10 October 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how personal and technical information is handled when you visit ah-eisa.com (the "Site"). The Site is an independent professional website and does not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties.

2. Information Collected & Purpose

Information is collected only where strictly necessary to respond to legitimate inquiries or to maintain security and operational reliability:

Source Data Collected Purpose
Contact Form Name, email address, subject line, message text To evaluate, correspond, and respond directly to professional inquiries and investment discussions.
Server & Network Logs IP address, request timestamp, HTTP method, requested URI, User-Agent To protect the server infrastructure against abuse, automated attacks, and spam, and to enforce rate limiting.

3. How Your Information Is Used

  • Inquiry Correspondence: Contact details you submit are used solely to review and respond to your message.
  • Abuse Prevention & Security: Technical request metadata (such as IP addresses) is processed to mitigate malicious traffic, prevent form abuse, and maintain site availability.
  • No Commercial Sale: Submitted data is never sold, leased, or monetized.

4. Data Retention

  • Inquiries: Contact form submissions and email correspondence are retained for up to 12 months for record-keeping and follow-up, unless an ongoing professional engagement is established.
  • Security Logs: Server access and error logs containing technical metadata are rotated and retained for security diagnostics before being automatically purged.

5. Security Safeguards

All communication with the Site is encrypted in transit using modern HTTPS (TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3) with Strict Transport Security (HSTS) and restrictive security headers. Direct access to server backends is protected by network-level access controls and rate limiting.

6. Third-Party Services

The Site does not utilize third-party marketing pixels or cross-site tracking scripts. Infrastructure services (such as DNS and edge proxy caching) process basic technical network traffic strictly for routing and security purposes.
